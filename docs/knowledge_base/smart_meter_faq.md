# Smart meter FAQ

## How often does the meter send data?

The digital meter records consumption every 15 minutes. NorthSea Energy receives hourly values once a
day, the morning after. Values can arrive up to 3 days late when the mobile connection of the meter is
interrupted; the customer portal updates automatically when they arrive.

## Why is a day missing in my consumption chart?

A missing day usually means the meter could not send data. No consumption is lost: the meter stores the
values and sends them later. If data is missing for more than 3 days, contact customer service.

## My consumption looks abnormally high

NorthSea Energy automatically flags days where consumption is far above the usual pattern of your own
home, for example a spike six times higher than normal. Common causes are a new appliance, a heat pump
backup heater, or a faulty device. Flagged customers receive a notification within 2 days.

## Daylight saving time

On the last Sunday of March the day has 23 hours, and on the last Sunday of October it has 25 hours.
Daily totals and completeness checks take this into account.
