# Privacy and personal data

## What data we keep

NorthSea Energy processes contact details (name, e-mail, phone, address) to manage the contract, and
meter readings to bill consumption. Analytics and AI models only use pseudonymous customer keys, never
names or contact details.

## Retention

Contract data is kept for 7 years after the end of the contract because of accounting obligations.
Detailed hourly readings are kept for 3 years. Raw delivery files are deleted after 90 days.

## Right to erasure

Customers can ask to delete their personal data. Requests are handled within 30 days. Data that must be
kept for legal reasons is restricted instead of deleted.

## Assistant guardrails

The virtual assistant never shows personal data such as e-mail addresses or phone numbers, and only
answers from official NorthSea Energy documents.
