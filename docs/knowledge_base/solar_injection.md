# Solar panels and injection

## Injection compensation

Customers with solar panels and a digital meter are paid for every kWh they inject into the grid.
The injection compensation equals the hourly day-ahead market price minus a handling fee of 1.5 cent
per kWh. When the market price is negative, no compensation is paid for that hour.

## Self-consumption

Energy you produce and use yourself at the same moment never passes through the meter as import, so it
is the most valuable kWh. Running the washing machine, dishwasher or heat pump around midday increases
self-consumption and lowers the bill.

## Capacity tariff in Flanders

In Flanders the grid fee partly depends on the monthly peak: the highest 15-minute average power you take
from the grid each month. Spreading heavy loads such as EV charging and cooking avoids a high peak.

## Registering a new installation

New installations must be registered with the distribution system operator. NorthSea Energy activates
injection compensation within 10 working days after receiving the confirmation of the operator.
