# Tariff plans

NorthSea Energy is a fictional Belgian energy supplier used for this demo. It offers three
electricity tariff plans to residential customers in Flanders, Wallonia and Brussels.

## Variable tariff

The variable tariff is indexed every month on the wholesale market price. The energy price equals the
average day-ahead market price of the month plus a supplier margin of 12 cent per kWh. Customers can
leave the variable tariff at any time without a termination fee, with one month notice.

## Fixed tariff

The fixed tariff guarantees an energy price of 32 cent per kWh for 12 months, whatever happens on the
market. After 12 months the contract renews automatically at the fixed price valid at that moment.
Customers can switch to another plan at any time without a termination fee.

## Dynamic tariff

The dynamic tariff follows the hourly day-ahead market price plus a margin of 5 cent per kWh.
It requires a digital meter. Customers who shift consumption to cheap hours, for example by charging an
electric vehicle at night or running appliances when solar production is high, save the most.
Prices can be negative on very sunny and windy days.

## Grid and taxes

Distribution and transmission costs, levies and VAT are added on top of the energy price. In Belgium
the VAT rate on electricity for residential customers is 6 percent.
