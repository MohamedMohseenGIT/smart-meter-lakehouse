# Outages and compensation

## Reporting an outage

Power outages are handled by the distribution system operator, not by the supplier. Customers should
report an outage to their operator. NorthSea Energy customer service can help find the right operator
based on the postal code.

## Compensation for long outages

When an unplanned outage lasts longer than 8 hours, residential customers can request a compensation of
35 euro from the distribution system operator. The request must be made within 30 days after the outage.

## Planned works

Planned interruptions are announced at least 5 days in advance by letter or e-mail.
